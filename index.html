The Lumos Experience

Warm, comfortable & welcoming for every moment.

Nestled at 106 Harbord St, Lumos Café is thoughtfully crafted to be your home away from home. Whether you need a focused corner with high-speed Wi-Fi to study or work, or a relaxed table to catch up with friends over fresh coffee, our doors are open with true warmth.

We blend traditional European café charm with rich mahogany wood textures, comfortable seating, and ambient lighting. Pair your favorite espresso or herbal tea with our daily selection of fresh pastries and desserts.
